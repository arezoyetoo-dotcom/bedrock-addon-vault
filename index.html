How to Install .mcaddon on Mobile & Windows

Bedrock add-ons are distributed in the standard .mcaddon format which installs directly into Minecraft with one tap:

1

Tap Direct Download

Click the green Download .mcaddon button on any mod card to save the file to your device.

2

Open with Minecraft

Tap the downloaded file from your browser downloads or Files app. Minecraft will automatically launch and import the pack.

3

Activate in World

Go to your World Settings ➔ Behavior Packs (and Resource Packs if applicable) ➔ Activate the pack ➔ Enable Beta APIs in Experiments!
